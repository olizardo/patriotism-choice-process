## Data Cleaning & Scale Construction

All core data-cleaning decisions are reproducible from `generate_tables.R`:

✓ **Dichotomization logic** (lines 41–44)
- Pride items: 1 if response == 1 ("very proud"), 0 otherwise
- U.S. Pride Scale: sum of 10 dichotomized items (0–10)

✓ **Nationalism scale** (lines 74–79)
- Original scale: 1–5 Likert items
- Reverse-coded: 6 - mean of raw items
- Result: higher values = higher nationalism

✓ **Continuous Pride Scale** (lines 70–73, newly added)
- Mean of original pride items (not dichotomized)
- Range: 1–4

✓ **Missing data handling** (lines 36, 48–51)
- Pride scale: listwise deletion if any item missing
- Nationalism scale: mean with `na.rm = TRUE` (pairwise)
- Memberships: codes 98, 99, -1 treated as NA
- Survey weights: respondents with missing weights excluded
