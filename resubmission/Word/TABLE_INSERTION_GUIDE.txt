MANUAL TABLE INSERTION GUIDE FOR WORD DOCUMENT
===============================================

The Word conversion (manuscript_R1.docx) currently only shows Table 1 (Descriptive Statistics).
Tables 2-4 need to be manually inserted. Here's how:

TABLE 2: Predictors of Nationalism Scale (1996 vs 2004)
Location: After the first paragraph discussing nationalism results
- File: ../../Tabs/tbl-nationalism.tex
- Cross-reference: \ref{tab:nationalism}
- Placement: After "Table \ref{tab:nationalism}" text

TABLE 3: Effects of Education on U.S. Pride Scale (1996 vs 2004)
Location: After paragraph discussing education reversal
- File: ../../Tabs/tbl-regression-educ.tex
- Cross-reference: \ref{tab:educ}
- Placement: After "Table \ref{tab:educ}" text

TABLE 4: Predictors of U.S. Pride (2004 sample only)
Location: After paragraph discussing voluntary membership effects
- File: ../../Tabs/tbl-regression-full-2004.tex
- Cross-reference: \ref{tab:full2004}
- Placement: After "Table \ref{tab:full2004}" text
